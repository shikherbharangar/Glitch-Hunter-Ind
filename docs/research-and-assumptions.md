## Engineering hypotheses, not externally verified facts

- VIGIL weights, freshness half-life/decay, history thresholds, confidence factors and alert thresholds are provisional. They are not calibrated probabilities or proven optimal parameters. See `vigil.md`.
- Query priority is a small-sample heuristic combining yield, discoveries, age, false-positive feedback and cost. It is not an optimal search policy. Twenty percent of ticks select the least-run eligible query.
- Category phrases extracted from observed titles are labelled **Inferred**, not verified marketplace taxonomy. Model-like tokens and variants generate follow-up queries without claiming a product match.
- Cross-market product merging requires explicit matching complete configuration and brand. Unknown configurations stay scoped to their original listing. Text similarity alone never establishes identity.
- Search results establish indexed listing leads, not live price, stock, seller, cart, checkout, or seller acceptance.
